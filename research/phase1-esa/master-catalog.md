# VerdictTrace — consolidated literature search and master catalog

Search date: **16 November 2026**. Focus: 2025–2026, retaining older relevant references from the supplied audit.

## Results and limits

**97 catalog entries: 70 inherited entries plus 27 newly located entries.** Of these, 96 are paper/preprint/survey/technical-paper records and one is the standalone MINIDS dataset record (#47). This is a count of catalog records, not a claim that all 96 papers have been independently verified. A separate ITU recommendation is listed below and is excluded from the count.

All 70 original entries are retained with their original numbering. New entries are #71–97. “New” means new to the supplied 70-entry catalog; it does not necessarily mean published this month. Records were checked for repeated titles and identical URLs across these two sets; no duplicate was found. Preprint-to-journal equivalences still need a full bibliographic review.

**No fully identical end-to-end system was established by this search. This is not evidence that none exists.** Several papers strongly overlap individual modules and combinations. Similarity rankings are qualitative assessments of scope, not plagiarism judgments or measured similarity percentages.

The earlier audit mentions a separate 152-entry search, but does not reproduce those 152 records. They are not counted here or claimed to have been reconciled with this list. The complete list delivered here is the 97-entry consolidated catalog available from the attached audit and this search.

## Search coverage and method

Searches used combinations of CAN, V2X, cross-layer, intrusion, fault-versus-attack, causal attribution, provenance, missing observations, witness, Byzantine trust, forensic logging, and 2025/2026. Searches covered public web-indexed publisher and repository pages rather than authenticated searches inside every database.

Verification levels below distinguish retrieved primary metadata/abstracts from inherited candidates. Most studies have NOT undergone a complete method/results audit. Publisher existence checks do not validate a paper's results. A failed page retrieval does not prove a reference is false. Do not infer that a paper lacks a feature merely because its abstract omits it.

## Findings for VerdictTrace

The highest-value new comparison is #83 for incomplete V2X data, followed by #75 for fake braking and #82 for compromised roadside infrastructure. #91 is a very recent CAN detector published on 12 November, but its two streams are not the two domains of VerdictTrace. Likewise, “causal” in a transformer or temporal convolution can refer to excluding future inputs rather than identifying intervention-based causes.

The defensible research question remains whether matched CAN/external-V2X evidence improves fault-versus-attack attribution at comparable abstention coverage under missing/misleading sources, with a separately verifiable record of each decision. The search does not establish this as an unoccupied gap: #3, #12, #24, #75 and #83 require particularly close comparison.

For every core competitor, next extract: domains actually observed; benign fault labels; attack mechanism; clock assumptions; missingness model; uncertainty calibration; independent observer assumptions; key compromise; witness outage handling; payload retention; false-block measurement; dataset/code availability; and full experimental baselines. Only then claim a specific capability absent or a performance improvement.
